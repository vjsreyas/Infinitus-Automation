Put these files next to index.html (create the two folders):

images/vj.jpg                  your portrait, roughly square, 800px+ wide
videos/dashboard-demo.mp4      live dashboard (featured, top of "Work")
videos/comment-to-dm.mp4       comment -> reply -> DM
videos/x-tool.mp4              X comment-to-DM tool
videos/whatsapp-engine.mp4     WhatsApp lead engine (when ready)

Until a file exists, its slot shows "Video coming soon". Nothing else to change.

Video tips:
- MP4 (H.264), 60-90 seconds, under ~20 MB each. GitHub blocks files over 100 MB.
- Blur client names, phone numbers and any real lead data.
- No API keys, tokens, .env files or terminals showing secrets in frame.
- Remember the repo is public: anything you upload is public.
